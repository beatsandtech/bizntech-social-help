## How to Use the "Ask AI to Write" Functionality
**Category**: Ai Playground
**Reference ID**: how-to-use-the-ask-ai-to-write-functionality
**Overview**: 

### Instructions & Details:
The "Ask AI to Write" functionality in Bizntech Social allows you to effortlessly generate engaging and tailored content for your social media platforms. By providing clear instructions and guidance to our AI-powered system, you can create compelling posts that resonate with your audience. This article will walk you through the step-by-step process of using this powerful feature.

### Step 0: 

Choose the Social Network Before getting started, ensure that you have correctly integrated your desired social network. You can choose the network from the sidebar on the left. If you encounter any integration issues, refer to our "Integrations" article for assistance.

*Example: Choose "LinkedIn" as the social network for which you want to create the content.*

### Step 1:

 Define Your Topic In this initial step, provide a brief description of what you want to write about. Clearly communicate your topic to the AI system, allowing it to understand your intentions and generate relevant content.

*Example: "Engaging Tips for Boosting Social Media Engagement."*

### Step 2: 

Outline the Key Points In the second step, outline the main points you want to include in your content. Provide detailed instructions, important details, specific instructions, data, or any other relevant information that will guide the AI's content creation process.

*Example: *

- 
* Start with an attention-grabbing hook.*

- 
*Highlight the importance of creating valuable content.*

- 
*Share practical strategies for increasing engagement, such as using relevant hashtags and engaging with your audience.*

- 
*Emphasize the power of visual content and storytelling.*

- 
*Conclude with a call to action, encouraging users to like, comment, and share.*

### Step 3:

 Customize Your Copy Here, you have the option to customize the tone and style of your content. Choose a shade that aligns with your desired tone, such as humorous, formal, friendly, or informal. Additionally, select a format that best suits your content, such as discounts/rebates, practical guides, customer recommendations, breaking news, or featuring a product. Finally, choose a Call To Action (CTA) if desired, including options like "Download now," "Link to website," "Like the post," "Comment on the post," or "Tag a friend."

*Example:*

- 
*Tone: Friendly*

- 
*Format: How to Guides*

- 
*Call To Action (CTA): "Tag a friend"*

[*](assets/images/media_68.png)
### 
Step 4: 

Add/Lock Prompt and Create Another After reviewing the input, you can choose to "add" the prompt. This ensures that the AI doesn't make any further changes to the current copy.

However, if you wish to create additional copies based on the same or a different prompt, you can select the "Add" option. This allows you to input a new prompt and receive multiple copies at once, saving you time and effort.

Experiment with different variations and prompts to explore the full potential of the AI-powered content generation in Bizntech Social.

By utilizing the "Add" functionality, you can unlock a range of creative possibilities and receive multiple copies tailored to your needs, all in one go.

Example: 
​
Step 1: Define Your Topic "Creating a Healthy Work-Life Balance"

Step 2: Outline the Key Points Key Points:

- 
Introduction: Importance of work-life balance for overall well-being and productivity.

- 
Tips for Setting Boundaries: Establishing clear boundaries between work and personal life.

- 
Time Management Strategies: Efficiently managing tasks and prioritizing self-care.

- 
Benefits of Work-Life Balance: Improved mental health, increased job satisfaction, and better relationships.

- 
Inspiring Examples: Sharing stories or quotes from successful individuals who have achieved work-life balance.

Step 3: Customize Your Copy Customization:

- 
Tone: Scientific

- 
Format: Industry Tips and Tricks

- 
Call To Action (CTA): "Like the Post"
​
​
[](assets/images/media_69.png)
### 

Step 5: 

Generate Prompts and Go to the Editor Once you've provided all the necessary information in the previous steps, click on the "Generate Prompts" button. Bizntech Social's AI will now generate multiple copies based on your inputs. You'll be directed to the Editor, where you can review and refine the generated copies.

In the Editor, you have full control over the copies. You can make edits to enhance the content, such as adding more specific details, translating the copies into different languages, making them more concise, or even introducing new topics. Feel free to experiment and fine-tune the copies to align them perfectly with your desired social media message.

Remember, the Editor allows you to be creative and customize the generated content to suit your unique needs and preferences. Once you're satisfied with the final versions, you can proceed to publish or schedule the copies directly from the Editor, streamlining your social media content creation process.

With Bizntech Social's "Ask AI to Write" functionality and the powerful Editor, you'll have an efficient and flexible workflow for creating engaging social media content that resonates with your audience.

[](assets/images/media_70.png)

​

By following these simple steps, you can guide the AI to generate content that aligns with your vision and captivates your audience. Explore the endless possibilities of AI-powered content creation and elevate your social media strategy with Bizntech Social.*

## Introducing "Create from Images"
**Category**: Ai Playground
**Reference ID**: introducing-create-from-images
**Overview**: 

### Instructions & Details:
We're excited to introduce our latest feature, "Create from Images," designed to empower you with even more creative possibilities for your social media content. With this feature, you can transform your images into engaging, AI-generated copy for various social media platforms effortlessly. Let's dive into how it works:

[](assets/images/media_71.png)

​

**Step 1: Access the Playground**

To get started, head over to our Playground, your creative hub within Bizntech Social. Here, you can craft new documents and access a range of innovative tools.

**Step 2: Select "Create from Images"**

Within the Playground, choose the "Create from Images" functionality. This powerful tool opens up a world of opportunities for content creation.

**Step 3: Define Your Social Media Platform**

Select the social media platform for which you want to create content. Whether it's Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, or another, Bizntech Social has you covered.

**Step 4: Set the Tone**

In the same modal, you have the option to define the tone of your content. Tailor it to your brand's voice, whether you prefer something casual, formal, or somewhere in between.

**Step 5: Choose the Format and Call to Action (CTA)**

Customize your content further by selecting the format you desire, from short and snappy to long-form. You can also choose whether to include a call to action, making your content not only engaging but actionable.

**Step 6: Upload Your Images**

Now comes the creative part. Start uploading your images. Feel free to upload multiple images, knowing that Bizntech Social will generate content based on your selected prompts for each image.

**Step 7: Review and Edit**

Once Bizntech Social has worked its magic, you'll see the AI-generated copies. Review and edit them to align perfectly with your brand's message and style. You have full control to enhance and refine the content as needed.

**Step 8: Schedule Your Content**

With your content perfected, you're now ready to schedule it for publishing. Enjoy the peace of mind knowing that your social media strategy is in capable hands.

## How to Use Creative Studio
**Category**: Ai Playground
**Reference ID**: how-to-use-creative-studio
**Overview**: 

### Instructions & Details:
**Getting started**

**Open Creative Studio from the left sidebar under AI Images → Creative Studio.**

**Step 1 — Describe your creative**

**In the text area at the top, write a detailed description of what you want to generate. Be specific about the style, mood, and content you’re looking for.**

**Tips for better results:**

- 
Mention the topic, audience, and platform (e.g. “A bold carousel about productivity tips for entrepreneurs”).

- 
Include visual preferences like “vibrant gradients”, “clean typography”, or “minimalist layout”.

- 
The more detail you provide, the closer the result will match your vision.

**Step 2 — Upload a reference image (optional)**

**If you have a product photo or visual asset you want included, drag it into the upload area or click click to browse. Accepted formats: PNG, JPG, WEBP.**

**This is entirely optional. If you skip it, Creative Studio will generate the visual from scratch based on your description.**

**Step 3 — Choose a content type**

**Select one of three options:**

- 
**AI Decides** — lets the AI determine the best format.

- 
**Single Creative** — generates one standalone image.

- 
**Carousel** — generates a multi-slide set. Use the –/+ control to choose between 3 and 10 slides.

**Step 4 — Select an aspect ratio**

**Pick the ratio that matches your target platform:**

- 
**1:1** — Instagram feed, Facebook, LinkedIn.

- 
**4:5** — Instagram portrait posts.

- 
**9:16** — Stories, Reels, TikTok.

- 
**16:9** — YouTube thumbnails, landscape posts.

- 
**2:3** — Pinterest pins.

**Step 5 — Pick a visual style (optional)**

**Choose from a library of agency-grade styles. Click the style selector to browse options. Available styles include:**

- 
**Minimalist Clean** — white space with thin typography.

- 
**Bold Typography** — oversized, attention-grabbing text.

- 
**Gradient / Vibrant** — vivid gradient backgrounds.

- 
**Bold Hook 3D** — punchy 3D elements on pastel covers.

- 
**Bold Statement** — solid-color backgrounds with mixed fonts.

- 
**Glassmorphism** — frosted glass panels with translucent blur.

- 
**Neo-Brutalism** — raw geometric shapes with thick outlines.

- 
**AI Decides** — lets the AI choose the most fitting style for your prompt.

You can also use the search bar at the top of the style selector to find a specific style quickly.

**Step 6 — Add brand context (optional)**

**Toggle any combination of these options to keep your creatives on brand:**

- 
**Include brand context** — applies your brand’s identity and messaging.

- 
**Include brand logo** — places your logo on every generated image.

- 
**Use brand colors** — applies your brand’s color palette to the design.

Brand settings are pulled from your **Company Settings** in Bizntech Social. Make sure your brand identity is configured there before using this feature.

**Generating your creative**

**Once you’ve completed the steps above, click the Generate button at the bottom. The credit cost is displayed next to the button (e.g. “25 credits” for a single creative or “175 credits” for a 7-slide carousel).**

**Your results will appear on the Canvas — the preview area on the right side of the screen. Generation usually takes a few seconds.**

**Working with the Canvas**

**The Canvas is your design workspace where all generated creatives appear. You can zoom in and out using the controls at the bottom (or scroll), and navigate between slides in a carousel by scrolling horizontally.**

**From the Canvas toolbar you can:**

- 
**Save to Media** — sends all images to your Media Library for future use.

- 
**Download All** — downloads every image in the set to your device.

- 
**Use in Post** — opens the Post Composer with your creatives attached, ready to publish to any social network connected to your Bizntech Social account.

- 
**Create New** — starts a fresh generation from scratch.

**Editing individual images with AI**

**Click on any generated image to select it. An Edit Instructions panel will appear where you can type a natural-language instruction — for example:**

- 
“Change color to violet”

- 
“Make the text larger”

- 
“Replace the background with a sunset”

- 
“Remove the logo”

Click **Edit Image** to apply the change. The AI will regenerate that specific image while preserving the rest of your set.

You can also delete any image you don’t want and keep the rest.

**Credit usage**

**Creative Studio uses Media Credits from your Bizntech Social plan. The exact cost depends on the content type and number of images:**

- 
A single creative costs approximately 25 credits.

- 
A 7-slide carousel costs approximately 175 credits.

- 
Editing an existing image also consumes credits.

You can check your remaining credits in the **Media Credits** section in the left sidebar.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Can I edit a creative after generating it?**

**Yes. Click on any image and use the Edit Instructions panel to modify it with a text prompt. The AI will regenerate that specific image.**

**Which social networks can I publish to?**

**All networks connected to your Bizntech Social account via API. This includes Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), TikTok, YouTube, and more.**

**Do I need to set up my brand before using Creative Studio?**

**No, brand context is optional. However, for consistent branding across all creatives, we recommend configuring your brand identity, logo and colors in Company Settings first.**

**What’s the maximum number of slides in a carousel?**

**You can generate between 3 and 10 slides per carousel.**

**Can I choose an AI model?**

**Yes. You can switch the AI model from the top navigation bar. Creative Studio works with the model selected in your workspace settings.**
