## Social Hub

### Instructions & Details:
Introducing Bizntech Social's Social Hub: Streamline Your Social Media Communication

In today's fast-paced digital landscape, effective communication with your audience is essential for building strong relationships and providing exceptional customer support. Bizntech Social is proud to present the Social Hub feature, a powerful tool designed to revolutionize how you interact with your social media followers. With the Social Hub, you can efficiently manage and respond to messages and comments from your Facebook and Instagram accounts, empowering you to deliver personalized and improved community management.

### Messages

The Messages section of the Social Hub allows you to handle all your incoming messages from Facebook and Instagram in one centralized platform. Say goodbye to switching between multiple apps or platforms to stay on top of your social media conversations. With Bizntech Social's Social Hub, you can conveniently view, respond, and engage with your audience directly from a single location.

By consolidating your message management, you can provide timely and consistent responses, ensuring a seamless customer experience. The Social Hub enables you to handle customer inquiries, address support requests, and nurture leads, all within a user-friendly and efficient interface. Stay organized with a unified inbox and take control of your social media communication like never before.

Additionally, the Social Hub offers the ability to assign messages to specific team members within your company. This feature enhances collaboration and streamlines your customer support processes. Assigning messages allows you to delegate tasks, track progress, and ensure a personalized and efficient resolution for each customer interaction. With Bizntech Social's Social Hub, delivering exceptional support and managing your community becomes a breeze.

### Comments

The Comments section of the Social Hub brings all your post comments from Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn into one comprehensive view. You no longer have to visit each social media platform separately to keep track of comments on your posts. Now, you can efficiently manage and respond to comments directly within Bizntech Social's Social Hub.

By centralizing comments, you gain a holistic understanding of engagement on your social media posts. Whether it's a question, a compliment, or feedback, you can engage with your audience promptly and build stronger connections. Stay on top of your comment threads, reply to inquiries, and foster meaningful conversations, all while saving time and effort.

[](assets/images/media_408.png)

It's important to note that, currently, the Social Hub supports message and comment management for Facebook and Instagram only. Due to limitations with the APIs of other social media platforms, this feature is not available for those channels. However, our team is continuously exploring possibilities and working towards expanding the Social Hub's capabilities to include more platforms in the future.

In conclusion, Bizntech Social's Social Hub is a game-changer for managing your social media communication. With its unified inbox for messages and comprehensive comment management, you can effectively engage with your audience, provide top-notch support, and cultivate a thriving online community. Enjoy the convenience, efficiency, and enhanced collaboration offered by Bizntech Social's Social Hub as you take your social media presence to new heights.

You will be able to see new messages and comments on your account after integrating with Facebook and Instagram. Please note that messages and comments created before the integration will not appear in the Social Hub.

⚠️ For long-time users who had a Bizntech Social account before the launch of the Social Hub, it is necessary to refresh the Facebook and Instagram integrations to start seeing new messages and comments in the Social Hub. ⚠️

## Instagram 

Due to restrictions imposed by Meta, the user must always initiate the conversation. It is not possible to start conversations with end-users.

### Comments

- 
It is not possible to reply with Reels.

- 
Only posts that have been published less than 90 days ago will appear 
in this section.​

### Messages

- 
Once a user sends you a message you have 24 hours to respond before the session expires (Instagram API limitation)

- 
You can receive and send emojis as messages inside the conversation

- 
You can receive stickers, but you cannot send them.

- 
You can receive GIFs, but you cannot send them.

- 
Due to Instagram API limitations, it is not possible to delete comments from Bizntech Social.

- 
It is not possible to receive or send multimedia files.

- 
It is not possible to receive or send audio messages.

- 
It is not possible to receive or send emojis inside a selected message.

- 
It is not possible to reply to specific, previously selected messages. 

## Facebook 

Due to limitations imposed by Meta, the user must always initiate the conversation. It is not possible to start conversations with users.

### Comments 

- 
It is not possible to reply with an avatar, multimedia files, GIFs, or stickers.

- 
Comments containing stickers or multimedia files will appear as empty messages in Bizntech Social.

- 
Only posts that have been published less than 90 days ago will appear 
in this section.​
​

### Messages

- 
Once a user sends you a message you have 24 hours to respond before the session expires (Facebook API limitation)

- 
You can receive and send emojis as messages inside the conversation

- 
You can receive static stickers (animated stickers will appear as static), but you cannot send them.

- 
You can receive GIFs, but you cannot send them.

- 
You can receive multimedia files, but you cannot send them.

- 
Due to Facebook API limitations, it is not possible to delete comments.

- 
It is not possible to receive or send audio messages.

- 
It is not possible to receive or send emojis inside a selected message.

## Instagram 

Debido a restricciones impuestas por Meta, el usuario debe siempre iniciar la conversación. No es posible iniciar conversaciones con los usuarios finales.

### **Comentarios**

- 
No es posible responder con Reels.

- 
Sólo aparecerán en esta sección los posteos publicadas hace menos de 90 días.

### **Mensajes**

- 
Una vez que un usuario te envía un mensaje, tienes 24 horas para responder antes de que caduque la sesión (limitación de la API de Instagram)

- 
Puedes recibir y enviar emojis como mensajes dentro de la conversación.

- 
Puedes recibir stickers, pero no puedes enviarlos.

- 
Puedes recibir GIFs, pero no puedes enviarlos.

- 
Debido a limitaciones de la API de Instagram, no es posible eliminar comentarios desde Bizntech Social.

- 
No es posible recibir ni enviar archivos multimedia.

- 
No es posible recibir ni enviar mensajes de audio.

- 
No es posible recibir ni enviar emojis dentro de un mensaje seleccionado.

- 
No es posible responder a mensajes específicos previamente seleccionados.

## Facebook 

Debido a limitaciones impuestas por Meta, el usuario debe siempre iniciar la conversación. No es posible iniciar conversaciones con los usuarios.

### **Comentarios**

- 
No es posible responder con un avatar, archivos multimedia, GIFs o stickers.

- 
Los comentarios que contengan stickers o archivos multimedia aparecerán como mensajes vacíos en Bizntech Social.

- 
Sólo aparecerán en esta sección los posteos publicadas hace menos de 90 días.

### **Mensajes**

- 
Una vez que un usuario te envía un mensaje, tienes 24 horas para responder antes de que caduque la sesión (limitación de la API de Facebook)

- 
Puedes recibir y enviar emojis como mensajes dentro de la conversación.

- 
Puedes recibir stickers estáticos (los stickers animados aparecerán como estáticos), pero no puedes enviarlos.

- 
Puedes recibir GIFs, pero no puedes enviarlos.

- 
Puedes recibir archivos multimedia, pero no puedes enviarlos.

- 
Debido a limitaciones de la API de Facebook, no es posible eliminar comentarios.

- 
No es posible recibir ni enviar mensajes de audio.

- 
No es posible recibir ni enviar emojis dentro de un mensaje seleccionado.
