## Understanding TikTok Account Types and Switching Between Them
**Category**: Troubleshooting
**Reference ID**: understanding-tiktok-account-types-and-switching-between-them
**Overview**: 

### Instructions & Details:
English Version 🇺🇸

TikTok offers two main types of accounts: Business and Personal. It's important to note that due to TikTok's API limitations, each account type provides access to different features within Later.

Please be aware that TikTok previously had a third account type (Creator), but it has been removed. If you're unsure about your account type or wish to switch, read on to find out how.

**Checking Your TikTok Account Type**

To determine your TikTok account type, follow these steps:

- 
Open the TikTok mobile app and go to your profile.

- 
Tap on the ☰ icon in the top right corner.

- 
Select "Settings and privacy."

- 
Choose "Manage account."

- 
Look under "Account control":

If it suggests "Switch to Business Account," you currently have a Personal Account.

- 
If it proposes "Switch to Personal Account," you're using a Business Account.

Please note that TikTok's API restrictions require a Business Account to access various Later features, including Auto Publish, Analytics, and Conversations.

**Switching to a TikTok Business Account**

To transition to a TikTok Business Account, adhere to these steps:

Important: TikTok Business Accounts are public, making your posts visible to all TikTok users.

- 
Launch the TikTok mobile app and access your profile.

- 
Tap on the ☰ icon in the top right corner.

- 
Choose "Settings and privacy."

- 
Select "Manage account."

- 
Under "Account control," tap on "Switch to Business Account."

- 
Proceed by tapping "Next."

- 
Opt for the category that best suits your TikTok account.

After this, you can also choose to display your business email on your TikTok profile. It's worth mentioning that switching back to a Personal Account is possible at any time by following the same steps and selecting "Switch to Personal Account." However, this move will result in losing access to TikTok Business Account features.

**Understanding the Distinctions Between TikTok Business & Personal Accounts**

TikTok's API restrictions contribute to the differing capabilities of each account type:

**TikTok Business Accounts**

These accounts enjoy enhanced capabilities granted by TikTok's API, which translates to more features in Later.

What Business Accounts Can Do:

- 
Auto Publish videos on TikTok.

- 
Access TikTok Analytics (available in current paid plans only).

- 
Manage TikTok comments using Conversations (available in current paid plans only).

- 
Display a business email in their TikTok profile.

**TikTok Personal Accounts**

Personal Accounts, while having the ability to go private on TikTok, have fewer API-granted capabilities.

What Personal Accounts Can Do:

- 
Publish videos on TikTok using notification publishing.

- 
Set their account to private on TikTok.

However, Personal Accounts lack the ability to Auto Publish videos, access TikTok Analytics, or manage TikTok comments via Conversations. Switching to a TikTok Business Account is advisable if you wish to utilize these features in Later (refer to the steps mentioned earlier).

Para cambiar a una Cuenta Comercial en TikTok, sigue estos pasos:

## Diferencias entre las Cuentas Comerciales y Personales de TikTok

Las restricciones de la API de TikTok contribuyen a las distintas capacidades de cada tipo de cuenta:

## Cuentas Comerciales de TikTok

Estas cuentas disfrutan de capacidades mejoradas otorgadas por la API de TikTok, lo que se traduce en más funciones en Later.

**Lo que pueden hacer las Cuentas Comerciales:**

- 
Publicar videos automáticamente en TikTok.

- 
Acceder a las analíticas de TikTok (disponible solo en planes pagos actuales).

- 
Gestionar comentarios en TikTok mediante Conversaciones (disponible solo en planes pagos actuales).

- 
Mostrar un correo electrónico de negocio en su perfil de TikTok.

## Cuentas Personales de TikTok

Las Cuentas Personales, aunque pueden hacer su cuenta privada en TikTok, tienen menos capacidades otorgadas por la API.

**Lo que pueden hacer las Cuentas Personales:**

- 
Publicar videos en TikTok utilizando la publicación por notificación.

- 
Configurar su cuenta como privada en TikTok.

Sin embargo, las Cuentas Personales no pueden publicar videos automáticamente, acceder a las analíticas de TikTok ni gestionar comentarios en TikTok mediante Conversaciones. Si deseas utilizar estas funciones en Later, es recomendable cambiar a una Cuenta Comercial de TikTok (consulta los pasos mencionados anteriormente).

## Troubleshooting: Confirming Correct Level of Access for Your Facebook Page
**Category**: Troubleshooting
**Reference ID**: troubleshooting-confirming-correct-level-of-access-for-your-facebook-page
**Overview**: 

### Instructions & Details:
English Version 🇺🇸

#### 

If you're experiencing issues connecting your Facebook Page, it's important to ensure that you have the correct level of access. Follow the steps below to confirm your admin permissions:

**Confirming Your Admin Permissions:**

- 
**Navigate to Your Facebook Page:** Start by going to your Facebook Page.

- 
**Access Page Settings:** Click on "Settings & Privacy," then select "Settings."

- 
**Check New Pages Experience:** Inside the settings, find "New Pages Experience" and click on it.

- 
**Verify Page Access:** In the "Page Access" section, check if there is a Business Account Manager listed.

If you see a Business Account Manager listed, you will need to confirm that you have "Full Control" admin permissions in the Facebook Business Manager.

- 
**Confirm Full Control Access:** To confirm "Full Control" access, go to **[Facebook Business Manager](https://business.facebook.com/settings)**, then navigate to "People." Ensure that "Full Control" access is listed under your Facebook name.

Select your business account.

- 
Navigate to "People" and ensure that "Full Control" access is listed under your Facebook name.

- 
**To grant access to individuals who work with you:**

Click "Add People" in the "People" section.

- 
Enter the email addresses or names of the individuals you wish to grant access to.

- 
Assign appropriate roles and permissions for each individual.

- 
Click "Add" to give them access to the account.

That's it! By following these steps, you can verify that you have the necessary permissions to manage connections for your Facebook Page in the New Page Experience.

If you encounter any issues or have further questions, feel free to reach out to our customer support team. We're here to help!
