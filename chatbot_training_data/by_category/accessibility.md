## How to Add the Bizntech Social App to Your Phone's Home Screen
**Category**: Accessibility
**Reference ID**: how-to-add-the-bizntech-social-app-to-your-phone-s-home-screen
**Overview**: 

### Instructions & Details:
### iOS users. 

Here's a step-by-step guide on how to add the Bizntech Social web app to the home screen of their iPhones or iPads.

**Step 1:**  To begin the process of adding the Bizntech Social app to your home screen, open the Safari browser on your iPhone or iPad.

**Step 2:** In Safari, navigate to the Bizntech Social website. 

**Step 3: ** Once you're on the Bizntech Social website, locate and tap the Action button at the bottom center or top right of the Safari browser window. This button is often represented by a square with an arrow pointing upward.
[](assets/images/media_342.jpeg)
**Step 4:** Select "Add to Home Screen": From the options presented in the action menu, scroll down until you find the "Add to Home Screen" option. Tap on it to proceed.

[](assets/images/media_343.jpeg)

**Step 5**: Give the Web App a Name: A pop-up window will appear, allowing you to give the Bizntech Social web app a name. You can use the default name or enter a custom name of your choice that will appear on your home screen.

**Step 6:** Tap "Add": After entering the desired name, tap the "Add" button in the top right corner of the screen to finalize the process.

### **Android user:**

**Step 1**: Open Chrome on your Android device.

**Step 2: **Navigate to the website or web page associated with the Bizntech Social app.

**Step 3: **Once on the desired page, tap the menu icon located in the upper right-hand corner of the browser window (usually represented by three dots).

**Step 4:** From the menu that appears, select "Add to home screen."

**Step 5:** You'll be prompted to choose a name for the website shortcut. Enter a name that represents Bizntech Social.

**Step 6: **Finally, tap "Add," and Chrome will add the Bizntech Social app shortcut to your Android home screen.

[](assets/images/media_344.jpeg)

## Common API Key Errors

If when creating content you get an alert from Bizntech Social saying that it is impossible to create content, it's essential to troubleshoot common errors related to API key usage. Here are a few key areas to check:

- 
**Toggle Enabled:** Ensure that the feature requiring the API key is toggled on. If it's not enabled, the key won't function as expected.

- 
**Correct API Key:** Double-check that you've entered the correct API key. Even a small typo can prevent the API from functioning.

- 
**Available Credits:** Verify that you still have available credits for API usage. If you've exhausted your credits, the API will stop working until they're replenished.

- 
**Rate Limit:** If your API calls are being throttled, it might be necessary to increase your rate limit. This allows for more requests within a given timeframe.

By reviewing these common errors, you can quickly identify and resolve the issue, ensuring smooth content creation.

## Errores Comunes de Claves API Externas

Si al crear contenido recibes una alerta de Bizntech Social diciendo que es imposible crear contenido, es esencial revisar los errores comunes relacionados con el uso de la clave API externas. Aquí hay algunas áreas clave que debes revisar:

- 
**Interruptor Activado:** Asegúrate de que la función que requiere la clave API esté activada. Si no está habilitada, la clave no funcionará como se espera.

- 
**Clave API Correcta:** Verifica que hayas ingresado la clave API correcta. Incluso un pequeño error tipográfico puede impedir que la API funcione.

- 
**Créditos Disponibles:** Comprueba que aún tengas créditos disponibles para el uso de la API. Si has agotado tus créditos, la API dejará de funcionar hasta que los recargues.

- 
**Límite de Solicitudes:** Si tus solicitudes a la API están siendo limitadas, puede ser necesario aumentar tu límite de solicitudes. Esto permite más solicitudes dentro de un periodo de tiempo determinado.

Revisando estos errores comunes, puedes identificar y resolver el problema rápidamente, garantizando una creación de contenido fluida.

## X Premium
**Category**: Accessibility
**Reference ID**: x-premium
**Overview**: 

### Instructions & Details:
# English Version

Bizntech Social supports users on X (formerly Twitter) who have a premium paid account by recognizing their subscription status and enabling access to certain features associated with X Premium. One of the key benefits for premium users is the ability to create posts of up to 25,000 characters.

### Updating Your Subscription Information

If you subscribe to or cancel your X Premium subscription after integrating your account with Bizntech Social, it is important to update the integration. This ensures that X communicates your updated subscription status to Bizntech Social. Without this step, the following may happen:

- 
Changes to your premium status may not be recognized, which could affect your ability to use extended character limits, or on the other hand,

- 
You may be allowed to create posts of up to 25,000 characters but encounter an error when publishing them because you have canceled your X Premium subscription.

### Steps to Update Your Integration

- 
Open the [Integrations](https://bizntechstl.com) section in your Bizntech Social account.

- 
Locate your X (Twitter) account in the list of integrated platforms.

- 
Click on the "*Refresh Integration"* button to refresh the connection.

By following these steps, Bizntech Social will retrieve your current subscription status and apply any associated features automatically.
