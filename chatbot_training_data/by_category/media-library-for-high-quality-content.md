### Time-Saving Features

The media library also saves users' time by eliminating the need to search for visual content on other platforms. Instead, everything they need is available in one place, making the content creation process quicker and more efficient.

### How to Use Bizntech Social's Media Library

To access Bizntech Social's media library, users can click on the "Explore media" button in the calendar. From there, they can use the search bar to look up specific keywords or browse the library by category. Once they've found the media they want to use, they can add it to their content and schedule it for posting.

You can find DALLE·3, Stable Diffusion, and Fal Flux AI models in Bizntech Social's image generation engine. There is no correct way to create prompts for image generation. Bizntech Social understands people's natural language without having to make complex prompts. You can be as specific as you want or just write a single word.

The languages accepted for the prompts are the same ones that are [supported by Bizntech Social](#/article/languages-in-bizntech-social) at the playground section. If you don't find yours, just let us know so we can add it.

- 
Square 1:1 (available in all models)

- 
Landscape 16:9 (available in all models)

- 
Landscape 4:3 (available in Stability, Fal Flux, and Ideogram)

-

-

-

- 
Portrait 2:3 (available in Stability e Ideogram)

Some formats are not available on certain models due to model limitations

Asking Bizntech Social to generate images with a specific word or phrase can be very useful; however, be aware that depending on the model, text creation may not be its strongest suit and may generate inaccurate text, especially with proper nouns.

The same can happen when asking for images showing the faces of human beings. You should not be surprised to see unusual distortions and strange faces in the images.

## Useful Tips

- 
**Clear and Detailed Descriptions:** Provide clear and detailed descriptions of what you want. The more information you provide, the better the model's understanding will be.

- 
**Experiment with Concepts:** Try describing abstract concepts or unconventional ideas. Bizntech Social's providers are known for their ability to generate creative and unique images.

- 
**Adjust the Length of the Description:** Experiment with the length of the descriptions. Sometimes, shorter descriptions can be more effective, while others may require additional details.

- 
**Explore Different Image Sizes:** Play with the size of generated images. You can experiment with different resolutions to suit your needs.

- 
**Refine with Iterations:** If the first generation is not exactly what you're looking for, iterate by adjusting and refining the description for better results.

- 
**Experiment with Animation:** Experiment with descriptions that suggest movement to achieve interesting results.

## Consejos Útiles

- 
**Descripciones Claras y Detalladas:** Proporciona descripciones claras y detalladas de lo que deseas. Cuanta más información proporciones, mejor será la comprensión del model.

- 
**Experimenta con Conceptos:** Intenta describir conceptos abstractos o ideas poco convencionales. Los proveedores de Bizntech Social son conocidos por su capacidad para generar imágenes creativas y únicas.

- 
**Ajusta la Longitud de la Descripción:** Experimenta con la longitud de las descripciones. A veces, descripciones más cortas pueden ser más efectivas, mientras que otras pueden requerir detalles adicionales.

- 
**Explora Diferentes Tamaños de Imagen:** Juega con el tamaño de las imágenes generadas. Puedes experimentar con diferentes resoluciones para adaptarlas a tus necesidades.

- 
**Refina con Iteraciones:** Si la primera generación no es exactamente lo que buscas, itera ajustando y refinando la descripción para obtener mejores resultados.

- 
**Experimenta con Animación:** Experimenta con descripciones que sugieren movimiento para lograr resultados interesantes.
