# English Version

Creating high-quality content is only half the battle when it comes to social media marketing. ## How to Schedule Posts

Once you've created your content in Bizntech Social's Ai Playground, you can easily schedule it for posting using Bizntech Social's calendar feature. The calendar displays all your scheduled posts in a user-friendly interface, organized by dates, allowing you to see and manage all your posts at a glance.

To schedule a post, simply drag and drop your content into the desired date and time slot on the calendar. Bizntech Social will prompt you to confirm the post before it is scheduled, giving you the option to edit or delete the post if needed.

[](assets/images/media_73.png)

## Automating Post Scheduling

In addition to scheduling posts manually, Bizntech Social also offers an automated post scheduling feature. By analyzing the performance of your social media accounts, Bizntech Social is able to determine the best times to post your content for maximum engagement and reach.

To automate post scheduling, simply select the social media platform you want to post to, and Bizntech Social will automatically generate a posting schedule based on your account's performance data. This feature saves you time and ensures that your content is posted at the optimal time for the best engagement.

[](assets/images/media_74.png)

## Using Media Content to Enhance Your Posts

To enhance the quality of your posts, Bizntech Social provides a variety of media content, including images, videos, and gifs related to your brand or product. You can easily access these media files within the calendar interface, making it easy to add engaging media content to your posts.

In addition to Bizntech Social's media content, you can also import images and videos from Google Drive or Dropbox, allowing you to use custom designs created by your team to accompany your written content.

[](assets/images/media_75.png)

## Conclusion

Bizntech Social's post scheduling and  automation feature is a powerful tool that streamlines the content creation process, ensures that your content is posted at the optimal time, and provides a variety of media content to enhance your posts. By integrating with all your preferred social media platforms, Bizntech Social makes it easy to implement an effective social media marketing strategy.

**Best Times to Post on Facebook:**
