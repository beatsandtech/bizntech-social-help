## Managing multiple social media accounts on Bizntech Social

**Overview**: 

### Instructions & Details:
# English Version

Managing multiple social media accounts can be a daunting task, but with Bizntech Social, it becomes a breeze. Bizntech Social offers the Pro plan and the Agency plan, which allow multiple users to create and schedule content across various social media accounts. Additionally, Bizntech Social allows users to manage multiple companies, which can represent products or services, each with their own social media accounts.

To manage multiple social media accounts on Bizntech Social, follow these steps:

- 
Sign up for the Pro or Agency plan on Bizntech Social

- 
Complete the onboarding process for each company you manage

- 
Connect/integrate each company's social media accounts with Bizntech Social

- 
Have users create and schedule content for each company's social media accounts

- 
View metrics and reports for all social media accounts managed by your users

By following these steps, managing multiple social media accounts becomes a breeze with Bizntech Social.

## How to connect to a different Social Media Account
**Category**: Integrations
**Reference ID**: how-to-connect-to-a-different-social-media-account
**Overview**: 

### Instructions & Details:
# English Version

This is most likely because you have that account open in your browser, which saves cookies to keep the session open. You can easily check this by opening a new tab in your browser right now and typing in the URL of the social network you're trying to connect to, for example: facebook.com. You'll see that you're already logged in. 

Bizntech Social works in the same way, which is why when you remove the integration of a social network and reconnect it, it connects back to the same account.

But don't worry, it's very easy to sort this out. Below are two options:

## Option A

- 
Open a new tab in the same browser you are using for Bizntech Social.

- 
Enter the URL of the social network where you want to change the account, for example, tiktok.com (if the social network is Instagram, you should go to facebook.com since IG is managed through your personal FB account).

- 
You'll see that you're logged in. Log out of the session.

- 
Go to Bizntech Social's [integrations section](https://bizntechstl.com) and disconnect the social network.

- 
In the same section of Bizntech Social, re-integrate the social network.

- 
This time it should ask you to log in with your username and password. Log in with the correct account this time.

## Option B

- 
Open an **incognito** window in your browser. 

- 
Sign in to Bizntech Social. 

- 
Go to the [Integrations section](https://bizntechstl.com) and disconnect the social network you are trying to change. 

- 
In the same section of Bizntech Social, re-integrate the social network. 

- 
This time it should ask you to log in with your username and password. Log in with the correct account.

If your **personal Facebook** account logged into Bizntech Social is correct but you're not seeing your Instagram pages or accounts listed as options, then I recommend reading these two articles:

​[Confirm the correct level of access to your Facebook page](#/article/troubleshooting-confirming-correct-level-of-access-for-your-facebook-page)

[How to add a Facebook page to your professional Instagram account](#/article/how-to-add-a-facebook-page-to-your-professional-instagram-account)

It's important to understand that both the Facebook page and the professional Instagram account are linked to the same **personal Facebook **account. That's why when you first log in to Instagram or Facebook, it asks you to connect your **personal Facebook **account.

It's important to understand that both the Facebook page and the professional Instagram account are linked to the same **personal Facebook **account. That's why when you first log in to Instagram or Facebook, it asks you to connect your **personal Facebook **account.

##

First, make sure you don't have any issues with your Facebook page access level. If you can connect your Facebook page to Bizntech Social that means that you have the correct level of permission. Meta requires all Instagram accounts for the following two things:

- 
It should be a Business/professional account

- 
The business account must be connected to a Facebook page.

Here are some articles that might help you with the connection:

- 
[Convert Your Instagram Profile to a Business Profile](#/article/step-by-step-guide-how-to-convert-your-instagram-profile-to-a-business-profile)

Here is an article that can help you with that process:

[Delete Bizntech Social Integration from Facebook](#/article/how-to-fix-error-200-on-facebook)
