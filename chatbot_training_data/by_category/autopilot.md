## How to Create an Autolisting Rule

To automate your posts effectively, you need to have an organized tagging system in place. For this guide, we'll walk you through creating a rule to post every Monday morning on LinkedIn, ensuring your followers are reminded of your presence as the new workweek begins.

## Step-by-Step Guide

- 
**Ensure Your Posts are Tagged:**

Before creating rules, make sure your posts are properly tagged. If you need to review how to tag posts, refer back to our TAGS feature guide.

- 
**Creating the Rule:**

Navigate to the Autolisting Rules section in your dashboard.

- 
Click on "Create Rule."

- 
**Name Your Rule:**

Give your rule a descriptive name, such as "Monday LinkedIn Posts."

- 
**Select Tags:**

Choose the tags you've assigned to the posts you want to automate. For our example, select tags related to professional content for LinkedIn.

- 
**Set Schedule:**

Select the day and time you want the posts to go live. For our scenario, choose Monday morning.

- 
Optionally, you can add a delay to the post.

- 
**Confirm the Rule:**

Click "CONFIRM" to finalize your rule.

[](assets/images/media_412.png)

## Managing Your Autolisting Rule

Once created, you will see your rule in the dashboard marked as "Paused."

- 
**Activate the Rule:**

To activate the rule, click the "Play" button.

- 
**Pause or Edit:**

You can pause the automation at any time by clicking "Pause."

- 
To make changes to your rule, click "Edit."

[](assets/images/media_413.png)
