## 5. Troubleshooting Playbooks for Common Issues

### Issue 1: Facebook Error 200 (Permissions Error)
- **Root Cause**: The user's Facebook account lacks "Manage Page" or full admin access, or permissions were unchecked during the OAuth connection.
- **Resolution**:
  1. Go to Facebook.com -> **Settings & Privacy** -> **Settings** -> **Business Integrations**.
  2. Locate the Bizntech Social integration and ensure ALL page permissions are granted.
  3. Return to **Bizntech Social** -> **Integrations**, disconnect the Facebook Page, and re-connect it, checking all boxes for access permissions.

### Issue 2: Error 10 (User is Not an Instagram Business)
- **Root Cause**: The Instagram account is set as a Personal account or disconnected from its Facebook Page.
- **Resolution**:
  1. In the Instagram mobile app, navigate to **Settings** -> **Account Type and Tools** -> **Switch to Professional Account**.
  2. Select either **Business** or **Creator**.
  3. Re-link the account in Meta Business Suite, then re-connect in Bizntech Social.

### Issue 3: Screen is Showing Up Gray / White Screen on Load
- **Root Cause**: Stale local storage or browser extension conflict (ad blockers, script blockers).
- **Resolution**:
  1. Perform a hard refresh (`Ctrl + F5` on Windows or `Cmd + Shift + R` on Mac).
  2. Clear browser cache and cookies for `bizntechstl.com`.
  3. Test in an Incognito/Private window. If resolved, disable conflicting extensions.

### Issue 4: Pinterest Publishing Errors
- **Root Cause**: Missing board selection, expired token, or attempting to publish image dimensions unsupported by Pinterest.
- **Resolution**: Ensure the post includes a specific destination board selected and that images meet standard vertical (2:3) aspect ratio requirements.

1. **Billing, Subscription Changes, or Refund Requests**:
   - Provide the policy summary: Subscriptions are managed directly via account settings; refund requests are evaluated according to the terms of service.

2. **Account Deletion / Data Requests**:
   - Guide them to **Settings** -> **Advanced**, and clarify that data deletion is permanent.
3. **Unresolved Technical Bugs**:
   - Request the user's browser version, operating system, and screenshot of the error, then provide:
